# Fill Tool User Guide - Professional Color Filling & Gap Detection\n\n## Quick Start\n\n### Using the Paint Bucket Fill Tool\n\n1. **Click the Fill Button (🎨)** in the bottom toolbar\n   - Status changes to \"FILL MODE\"\n   - Colors are no longer automatically selected\n\n2. **Select Your Color**\n   - Professional Color Picker dialog appears\n   - Choose from 24 professional colors\n   - Or create custom color using RGB sliders\n   - Or enter exact hex value\n   - Click \"Apply\" to confirm\n\n3. **Fill Your Diagram**\n   - Click on any closed area in your drawing\n   - Paint bucket fills that region\n   - Color is remembered for undo/redo\n\n4. **Undo/Redo if Needed**\n   - Click **↶ (Undo)** to revert last fill\n   - Click **⟲ (Redo)** to reapply fill\n\n---\n\n## Color Picker Explained\n\n### Professional Palette Section\n\n**24 Hand-Picked Professional Colors** organized by family:\n\n```\nReds & Pinks:        Purple Family:       Blue Family:\n🔴 Red              🟣 Purple box.\n\n### Recent Colors\n\n**Last 8 colors you used** are saved automatically:\n- Quick access to frequently used colors\n- Click to reuse a color\n- Updates every time you apply a color\n\n### RGB Sliders\n\n**Customize any color precisely:**\n\n```\nR: [████████████████] 200    (0-255)\nG: [████████████████] 100    (0-255)\nB: [████████████████] 150    (0-255)\n```\n\n**How to use:**\n- Drag slider left (darker) or right (brighter)\n- Value updates in real-time\n- Preview box shows result instantly\n\n**Common Recipes:**\n- Black: R=0, G=0, B=0\n- White: R=255, G=255, B=255\n- Gray: R=128, G=128, B=128\n- Pure Red: R=255, G=0, B=0\n\n### Hex Input\n\n**For designers & developers:**\n\n```\nHex: [#C86496]\n```\n\n**Enter a hex color code** like:\n- `#FF0000` (Red)\n- `#00FF00` get\n- Updates instantly as you adjust\n- White border helps see light colors\n\n---\n\n## Flood Fill in Action\n\n### What It Does\n\nThe **paint bucket tool** fills connected areas:\n\n```\nBefore:              After clicking inside:\n┌─────┐              ┌─────┐\n│ ░░░ │              │ 🔵🔵🔵 │\n│░░░░░│       →      │🔵🔵🔵🔵│\n└─────┘              └─────┘\n```\n\n### Smart Features\n\n✅ **Respects boundaries** - Only fills enclosed areas\n✅ **Stops at lines** - Won't cross drawn lines\n✅ **Color tolerance** - Fills similar shades\n✅ **Remembers action** - Can undo/redo\n\n### What Happens if Area Isn't Closed?\n\n❌ **Gap Detection alerts you:**\n\n```\n┌─────┐       User clicks to fill:\n│ ░░░ │       [INFO] Detected 2 gaps\n│░░ ░ │  →    Red markers show where\n└─────┘       gaps are located\n   ↑\nGaps here!\n```\n\n**Red highlights** mark unconnected points:\n- Complete your drawing\n- Add connecting lines\n- Try fill again\n\n---\n\n## Common Tasks\n\n### Task 1: Fill a Simple Shape\n\n1. Draw a closed rectangle using the **Line tool** (✏️)\n   ```\n   Click corner 1 → corner 2 → corner 3 → corner 4\n   ```\n\n2. Click **Fill button** (🎨)\n\n3. Choose **Blue** from palette\n   - Click the blue color square\n   - Click \"Apply\"\n\n4. Click **inside the rectangle**\n   - Rectangle fills with blue!\n\n### Task 2: Create Custom Color\n\n1. Click **Fill button** (🎨)\n\n2. **Adjust RGB sliders:**\n   - Red slider: drag to 200\n   - Green slider: drag to 100\n   - Blue slider: drag to 150\n   - Preview shows: salmon pink!\n\n3. Check **\"Recent Colors\"** section\n   - Your new color appears there\n   - Click it next time for instant access\n\n4. Click \"Apply\" and fill your shape\n\n### Task 3: Fix a Mistake\n\n1. Filled the wrong area?\n   - Click **↶ Undo** button\n   - Fill instantly reverts!\n   - ✓ Original color restored\n\n2. Oops, shouldn't have undone?\n   - Click **⟲ Redo** button\n   - Fill comes back\n\n3. Want to undo multiple fills?\n   - Click **↶ Undo** multiple times\n   - Each click reverts one fill operation\n   - Can redo them all with **⟲ Redo**\n\n### Task 4: Use Exact Hex Color\n\n1. Click **Fill button** (🎨)\n\n2. In the **Hex field**, type: `#38BDF8`\n   ```\n   Hex: [#38BDF8]\n   ```\n\n3. Press Enter\n   - Preview updates to cyan\n\n4. Click \"Apply\" → Fill your shape\n\n### Task 5: Multiple Colors in One Diagram\n\n1. Fill shape 1 with **Red** (#FF0000)\n   - Click fill → Choose red → Apply → Click shape\n\n2. Fill shape 2 with **Green** (#00FF00)\n   - Click fill → Choose green → Apply → Click shape\n\n3. Fill shape 3 with **Blue** (#0000FF)\n   - Click fill → Choose blue → Apply → Click shape\n\n**All three colors** appear in **Recent Colors**:\n- Quick switching next time!\n\n---\n\n## Color Palette Reference\n\n### Material Design Professional Colors\n\n| Color | Hex | Use Case |\n|-------|-----|----------|\n| 🔴 Red | #F44336 | Alerts, Errors, Warnings |\n| 🩷 Pink | #E91E63 | Highlights, Accents |\n| 🟣 Purple | #9C27B0 | Creative, Artistic |\n| 💜 Deep Purple | #673AB7 | Dark themes, Depth |\n| 🔵 Indigo | #3F51B5 | Professional, Trust |\n| 🟦 Blue | #2196F3 | Primary, Information |\n| 🔆 Light Blue | #03A9F4 | Secondary, Calm |\n| 🌊 Cyan | #00BCD4 | Active, Technical |\n| 💎 Teal | #009688 | Balance, Natural |\n| 💚 Green | #4CAF50 | Success, Growth |\n| 🟢 Dark Green | #45A049 | Natural, Earth tones |\n| 💚 Light Green | #8BC34A | Fresh, Spring |\n| 🟡 Lime | #CDDC39 | Vibrant, Modern |\n| 💛 Yellow | #FDD835 | Sunny, Happy |\n| 🟠 Orange | #FB8C00 | Warning, Energy |\n| 🧡 Deep Orange | #FF6F00 | Hot, Active |\n| 🟤 Brown | #795548 | Natural, Earthy |\n| ⚫ Gray | #9E9E9E | Neutral, Background |\n| ⬛ Dark Gray | #424242 | Text, Shadows |\n\n**Gaps are highlighted in:** 🔴 Coral Red (#FF6B6B)\n\n---\n\n## Tips & Tricks\n\n### 💡 Pro Tips\n\n1. **Test Small First**\n   - Draw a small test shape\n   - Practice fill before detailed work\n\n2. **Use Recent Colors**\n   - After 3rd use, color appears in Recent\n   - Saves clicking RGB sliders every time\n\n3. **RGB vs Hex**\n   - **RGB sliders** = visual adjustment\n   - **Hex input** = exact matching\n   - Use whichever feels faster!\n\n4. **Undo After Gap Detection**\n   - If gaps detected, draw closing lines\n   - Undo previous fill attempt\n   - Try again with closed shape\n\n5. **Color Naming**\n   - Write down hex codes for brand colors\n   - Example: Company blue = #0066CC\n   - Paste hex code instantly next time\n\n### ⚠️ Common Gotchas\n\n❌ **Fill won't work**\n- Shape might not be completely closed\n- Check for small gaps!\n- Gap detection shows where\n- Add connecting lines and retry\n\n❌ **Color looks wrong on canvas**\n- Preview box is accurate\n- Lighting/display affects appearance\n- Try #FFFFFF (white) for verification\n\n❌ **Undo reverts to original color**\n- This is correct behavior!\n- Shows what was replaced\n- Can redo to re-apply fill color\n\n---\n\n## Keyboard Shortcuts\n\n| Action | Keyboard | Mouse |\n|--------|----------|-------|\n| Fill Mode | F | Click 🎨 |\n| Undo | Ctrl+Z | Click ↶ |\n| Redo | Ctrl+Y | Click ⟲ |\n| Apply Color | Enter | Click \"Apply\" |\n| Cancel Picker | Esc | Click \"Cancel\" |\n\n---\n\n## Troubleshooting\n\n### Problem: \"Detected X gaps - shape may not be closed!\"\n\n**What it means:**\n- Your drawn shape has disconnected endpoints\n- Paint bucket can't fill properly\n\n**Solution:**\n1. Look for red highlighting on canvas\n2. Draw connecting lines to close gaps\n3. Try fill operation again\n\n### Problem: Fill only fills small area\n\n**What it means:**\n- You clicked near an incomplete boundary\n- Fill only goes to the nearest line\n\n**Solution:**\n1. Verify shape is closed\n2. Look for undrawn segments\n3. Complete the shape outline\n4. Try clicking more centrally\n\n### Problem: Can't change colors\n\n**What it means:**\n- Fill mode not active\n- Need to reopen color picker\n\n**Solution:**\n1. Click **Fill button** (🎨) again\n2. Wait for color picker to appear\n3. Select different color\n4. Click \"Apply\"\n\n### Problem: Undo reverts multiple operations\n\n**What it means:**\n- This is normal if filling same region\n- Each click adds to history\n\n**Solution:**\n1. Click **↶ Undo** one step at a time\n2. Or click **⟲ Redo** to get back\n3. Be intentional with undo count\n\n---\n\n## Feature Summary\n\n| Feature | Benefit | Status |\n|---------|---------|--------|\n| Paint Bucket Fill | Professional filling | ✅ Ready |\n| 24-Color Palette | Material Design colors | ✅ Ready |\n| RGB Sliders | Custom color creation | ✅ Ready |\n| Hex Input | Exact color matching | ✅ Ready |\n| Recent Colors | Quick access | ✅ Ready |\n| Gap Detection | Validate closure | ✅ Ready |\n| Undo/Redo | Non-destructive editing | ✅ Ready |\n| Color Preview | Real-time feedback | ✅ Ready |\n\n---\n\n## Related Help\n\n- **Getting Started**: See [QUICKSTART.md](QUICKSTART.md)\n- **All Features**: See [PROJECT_SUMMARY.md](PROJECT_SUMMARY.md)\n- **Design Details**: See [UI_DESIGN_GUIDE.md](UI_DESIGN_GUIDE.md)\n- **Technical Reference**: See [FILL_FEATURES_GUIDE.md](FILL_FEATURES_GUIDE.md)\n\n---\n\n## Support\n\n**Having trouble?**\n\n1.
